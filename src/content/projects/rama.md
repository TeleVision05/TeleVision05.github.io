## What it does

RAMA is a playable vertical slice set in ancient India, inspired by the journey of Lord Rama. You explore a 768 by 768 metre valley with a village, a river that falls over a cliff, a shrine, forest and ruins. You fight with a bow and a sword, complete three quests and talk to twelve villagers who keep daily schedules. It runs on macOS in Godot 4.7.2.

## How it works

- Nothing is downloaded. Textures and sound are synthesised in Python with numpy, and the music is synthesised from first principles.
- Terrain, trees, buildings, characters and weapons are generated as meshes in GDScript.
- The world is hand-composed rather than pure noise. The river descends about 45 metres across the map, so there is no flat sea level.
- The world heightfield is baked on first launch and cached on disk.
- Combat has a hold-to-draw bow with power scaling, a three-hit melee combo and an invulnerable dodge roll. A day and night cycle includes moonlight and stars.
- A 12-suite test gate drives the game headlessly, checks that every asset loads, and renders screenshots to disk so results can be inspected by eye. As of 2026-09-25, 11 of the 12 suites passed.

## What's next

- The performance suite has not been re-run since the 3D vegetation overhaul, and the new foliage has not been checked visually.
- Village draw calls are high, about 450 from characters alone, so characters need a distance level of detail.
- Remaining roadmap items are rendering and atmosphere, finishing characters and creatures, and more content.
- Building collision uses axis-aligned boxes, so a rotated hut blocks more ground than it occupies.
